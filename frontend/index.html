What is Yeşil İz?

Yeşil İz is a youth-led environmental initiative bringing high school students together to raise environmental awareness and take meaningful action for our planet.

What do we do?

Environmental Projects

We organize projects that create tangible environmental impact.

Awareness & Education

We encourage young people to learn about environmental issues and plan to organize events.

Volunteering

We bring high school students all around İstanbul together to take action.

Community

We build a growing, prestigious network of young people committed to a greener future.

Our Mission

The purpose of establishing Yeşil İz is to minimize the damage inflicted on nature while reaching as many volunteers as possible. By bringing high school students together under one roof, we aim to raise ecological awareness and empower them to make a meaningful, tangible difference in the world.

Our Vision

Our vision at Yeşil İz is to create a more sustainable future where environmental awareness becomes an integral part of everyday life, and where people view their responsibility toward nature not merely as a duty, but as a shared value. We strive to build a society in which young people are not only followers of change, but also its leaders.

Founders

Arya Döven and Sena Karapıçak

We are both studying in Üsküdar American Academy, planned to graduate in 2030. Meeting through the UAA Debate Club, we found that we had similar interests and passions, especially about the environment. We found that we were both very bothered by how dirty our city was. We thought that if we educated teenagers, we would promise a clean future for Türkiye. It became our mission to help young people like us understand how important the state of the environment is and take small steps to change it. Because even though we will start in İstanbul, we hope to globalize our ideals and empower every teenager to know that they can make a difference.

How Does the Volunteer System Work?

Being selected as a Yeşil İz volunteer does not automatically mean that you are considered an active volunteer. Your volunteer status is shaped by your participation in events and the active contribution you make throughout the process.

After each event you attend, you will receive a Volunteer Certificate documenting the amount of time you contributed to that event. At the end of each term, additional certificates will be awarded based on your overall participation:

  • 4 events: Active Volunteer Certificate
  • 7 or more events: Outstanding Volunteer Certificate

This system allows your time, commitment, and contributions to Yeşil İz to be formally recognized and documented throughout your volunteering journey.
